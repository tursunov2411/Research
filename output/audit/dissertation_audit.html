Executive Diagnosis

The dissertation is now structurally coherent and compiles, but it is not yet methodologically complete at the standard implied by its own text. The largest remaining risks are evidence provenance and overclaiming: IMF WEO is used as a label for projections it does not directly supply, the panel robustness check is still a limited cached fallback rather than the promised seven-country 2000-2023 specification, and several scorecards rely on author judgement without transparent raw inputs.

The best next move is not more prose. It is a reproducibility cleanup: align every empirical claim with a source table or script, downgrade claims that are proxy-based, and separate "completed analysis" from "future research".

CriticalMethodSources

IMF WEO is over-cited for variables it does not directly provide

Evidence: scripts/01_data_cleaning.R:230-257 hardcodes 2024-2026 manufacturing VA and FDI values, then labels all 2024+ rows as IMF WEO April 2025. The manuscript abstract and methodology describe data spanning 2010-2026 with IMF WEO projections.

Why it matters: WEO is appropriate for macro projections such as GDP and inflation, but not a direct source for manufacturing value added (% GDP) or FDI net inflows (% GDP). This is the most dangerous provenance issue in the dissertation.

Fix: Re-label these rows as author scenarios calibrated using WEO macro assumptions, or remove 2024-2026 from empirical claims and keep projections only in the scenario chapter.

CriticalMethod

Panel robustness check is still not the promised panel regression

Evidence: output/tables/panel_robustness.tex uses 5 countries and 65 observations over 2011-2023 after lagging FDI. It lacks Bangladesh and Cambodia, does not cover 2000-2023, and does not include intermediate-goods share, LPI, ECI, or country-specific reform-year binaries.

Why it matters: The current table is a useful stopgap but cannot bear the dissertation's stronger claim that cross-country identification resolves the small-N problem for the Northeast Asian mechanism.

Fix: Either complete the true 7-country 2000-2023 panel in R/plm, or explicitly describe the current table as a limited macro robustness check.

HighMethod

Negative intermediate-goods coefficient is now acknowledged but still needs final alignment

Evidence: output/tables/intermediate_diagnostics.tex shows VIFs up to 8.92, partial correlation controlling for trend of 0.147, and an insignificant first-difference coefficient of -11.912.

Why it matters: The new interpretation is more honest: the mechanism is compositional rather than aggregate. The conclusion and abstract still need a final pass to ensure they do not imply the aggregate coefficient supports the theory.

Fix: Carry the compositional framing into the abstract, central findings, and policy implications: Japan/Korea technology-intensive intermediates are the mechanism, not total intermediate share.

HighPresentationMethod

Vietnam reform-year baseline is inconsistent

Evidence: scripts/06_comparative_analysis.R uses Vietnam 2000-2008 as the reform window, while report_main.tex Figure 4 caption says year zero is Vietnam's 2007 WTO accession.

Why it matters: This changes the interpretation of the FDI and manufacturing gap. A 2000 baseline and a 2007 WTO baseline are both defensible, but they are not the same comparison.

Fix: Choose one baseline in figure, caption, script comments, and text. Best option: show both as a sensitivity note, with 2007 as the institutional-comparator baseline.

HighCodePresentation

archive/build_tsue.py should not be used for the final thesis

Evidence: The script opens missing references_expanded.bib, randomly selects citation keys, and generates a separate TSUE report with unsupported text and generic claims.

Why it matters: Random citation insertion is academically unsafe and can fabricate source support. It also risks producing a parallel manuscript inconsistent with report_main.tex.

Fix: Move this script to a clearly marked deprecated archive or rewrite it as a deterministic formatter that consumes the validated manuscript.

MediumSourcesMethod

SEZ scorecard needs raw indicators or should be labelled judgement-based

Evidence: chapters/ch_sez_analysis.tex and scripts/14_new_advanced_figures.R cite Farole, Uzinfoinvest, Vietnam EPZ Authority, and Thailand BOI, but the normalised scores are not traceable to a raw scoring table with formulas.

Why it matters: The reader cannot distinguish measured performance from expert assessment.

Fix: Add a scorecard data table with each raw metric, scaling rule, and source. If data are unavailable, rename the figure as an expert diagnostic matrix.

MediumSources

Enterprise Survey and firm-level constraint tests remain future work

Evidence: The repository has no Enterprise Survey microdata or SEZ/non-SEZ firm-level regression. The World Bank confirms Uzbekistan 2019 Enterprise Survey availability, and firm-level data require registered access.

Why it matters: Claims about firm constraints, SEZ underperformance, and binding constraints are currently macro/qualitative.

Fix: Put Enterprise Survey regressions in the future research section unless microdata are actually downloaded and analysed.

MediumMethod

Value-added trade proxy is discussed but not implemented

Evidence: The manuscript correctly notes Uzbekistan is absent from OECD TiVA and uses merchandise trade proxies. There is no Koopman-Wang-Wei style GVC participation calculation in the current outputs.

Why it matters: The "rightward but not upward" GVC positioning claim is plausible, but it remains a custom proxy rather than a recognised value-added participation index.

Fix: Either add a clearly labelled proxy GVC participation index or avoid invoking value-added trade language beyond limitations.

MediumSources

Political economy section is improved but still under-evidenced

Evidence: chapters/ch_constraints.tex mentions reform reversal risk and distributional losers, but does not yet incorporate CPI trends, EBRD transition assessments, or the 2023 constitutional referendum implications.

Why it matters: Institutional lock-in is one of the dissertation's top policy claims, so political economy evidence must be stronger than a paragraph.

Fix: Add a short subsection using Transparency CPI 2017-2023, EBRD Transition Report governance findings, and OSCE coverage of the 2023 referendum.

LowSources

CKU railway date should be precise

Evidence: The manuscript says construction begins in 2024. Official and trade-press sources place the launch/commencement ceremony on 27 December 2024, with some construction-phase details extending into 2025.

Fix: Say "officially launched on 27 December 2024" rather than generically "construction beginning in 2024".

Recommended Workplan

  2. Choose the Vietnam baseline and regenerate Figure 4/caption/text consistently.
  3. Either complete the full panel regression or downgrade the current table's claims.
  4. Push the compositional intermediate-goods finding into abstract, conclusion, and policy recommendations.
  5. Make judgement-based scorecards auditable with raw score tables.
